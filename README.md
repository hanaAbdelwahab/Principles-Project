Car Rental System
Welcome to the Car Rental System! 

This is a web-based platform that connects car renters with car owners. Users can easily browse and rent cars, while car owners can list their vehicles. Administrators have full control over the system and can manage users and car listings.

Features
For Users:
Browse a variety of cars (SUVs, Sedans, Electric cars, etc.).

Easily book cars based on your chosen dates and location.

Manage your profile and see your booking history.

For Car renters:
List your car(s) for rent with details (car model, price, availability).

Accept or decline booking requests made by users.

For Admins:
Manage users, car listings, and bookings.

View detailed system analytics, such as the total number of bookings, cars, and users.

Tech Stack
Backend: PHP for handling requests, MySQL for storing data.

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript for an interactive and responsive user interface.
How to Use
For Users:
Sign up for an account and log in.

Browse the available cars, and when you find one you like, book it for your desired dates.

Manage your bookings and view your profile at any time.

For Admins:
Admins can log in and manage everything – from car listings to user accounts and system stats.

You can see total cars, users, and bookings in your dashboard.
